Privacy Policy

Simple and clear. Like it should be.

🔒 No Data Collected

Rivora does not collect any personal information.

No name. No email. No location. Nothing.

💾 Where Are My Creations Stored?

Your creations are saved only on your device using your browser's built-in storage (called localStorage).

They never leave your device. We never see them.

🍪 No Cookies

Rivora uses no tracking cookies, no analytics cookies, and no advertising.

👦 Safe for Children

Rivora is built specifically for children and takes their privacy very seriously.

There are no accounts, no profiles, no social features.
